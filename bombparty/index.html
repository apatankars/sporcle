    How to get better

    1. Speed beats length. The fuse is hidden and keeps burning, so whoever hesitates usually eats the bomb. Type the first valid word you think of. Aim for under 2 seconds.
    2. Have a go-to word for every prompt. Good players don't search, they recall. Drill mode trains a reflex word per prompt and brings back the ones you miss until they stick.
    3. Ask where the letters sit. Before hunting, decide if the prompt is usually a start (UN, PRE), an ending (ING, TION, LY) or a middle (OU, EA). The coach tells you which after each prompt.
    4. Bend words you already know. Plurals and forms count as new words: if JUMP is taken, JUMPS, JUMPED, JUMPER and JUMPING are still free. Add -S, -ED, -ER, -ING, -NESS or RE-, UN-, OVER-.
    5. Keep backups. After a miss the same prompt often passes to the next player, and your favourite word might get used. Know two or three words for the common prompts.
    6. Hunt bonus letters only when it's cheap. Extra lives win long games, but only pick a longer word if it comes to you instantly. Keep a pocket word for each rare letter (J, Q, V, B, F…). The bonus panel lists some for the letters you're missing.
    7. Learn the prompts people blank on. Some prompts have hundreds of dictionary words but almost no everyday ones. The cheat sheet below lists them for your difficulty.

    A practice routine

    1. Warm up with one Drill set (Smart mix, 20 prompts).
    2. Play two or three rounds against Regular bots with the timer hidden.
    3. Finish with “Drill my weak spots” to lock in what you missed.
    4. Once your average is under 2.5s and you rarely miss, move up a difficulty.

    How BombParty works

    • The bomb shows a prompt of 2–3 letters. On your turn, type any word that contains them in that order, then press Enter.
    • A word can only be used once per round.
    • Answer and the bomb passes to the next player. The fuse keeps burning between turns and is hidden, so nobody knows when it will go off.
    • If it explodes in your hands you lose a life. The last player standing wins.
    • Use every letter in the bonus alphabet (A–V without K by default) across your words to earn an extra life, up to the max.
